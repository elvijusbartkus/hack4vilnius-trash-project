# Resident dashboard `/` (+ shared tokens, `/ops`)

Mode: Operate. Audience: hackathon judges on a big screen; the resident (Žygimantas) is the story. Task: skip in 1 click (reminder) or 2, book in 2, undo from the toast. Constraints: Lithuanian, light green + grey clay (pinned), transport typeface (pinned). No driver code in this repo. Working brand name "Trage". User rejected (with screenshots): route stop counts on the resident page, merged sections where nothing pops, and popups ("feel like scam ads").

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm logistics service: your next pickup is one clear decision, confirmed in place. Refuses merged manifest sheets, modal popups and the white-card SaaS dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Grey-clay ground; separate light-green panels with their own headings and gaps; one deep-green hero panel that pops. Barlow Semi Condensed for values and dates, Barlow for text, tabular figures. Status as a rectangular stamp. Authored SVG icons and a bin-with-check brand mark beside the "Trage" wordmark.

STORY: Žygimantas opens the page, sees "Rytoj išvežimas. Ar konteineris pilnas?" inline, taps "Ne, praleisti"; the hero date is struck and stamped PRALEISTA, "Kitas: spalio 30 d." appears; undo from the toast. Booking: tap a day, the day panel opens below the strip, tap "Užsakyti už 4 €".

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header (mark + Trage, address switcher, person). Left 8 columns: reminder notice, then the deep-green "Kitas išvežimas" panel with the date ~96px, stamp, two actions with inline confirm; then the 14-day strip panel. Right 4 columns: history, container, impact panels.

FORM: Route Manifest (seed key 2bd1f36b, my grounded list position 3), revised by user feedback into separated panels with inline confirmation. Signature interaction: stamp press on the hero; day panel opening under the strip.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
